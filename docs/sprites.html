Glance test — device-equivalent size

AC0.3. Top row is the sprite as it would appear on the 2.8″ panel (64×64 blitted at ×3 = 192px). Bottom row simulates looking at it from across the desk. Question: can you name each condition without reading the labels? Cover them and try.

If a condition is only nameable because of its label, that condition has failed — not the label.

What this page cannot show you (AC0.5). The 192px figure is arithmetic only — a 64×64 cell blitted at ×3. It is not a simulation of the device. Four things differ and none of them are reproducible here: the panel's brightness, its contrast, its reflectance under desk lighting, and your own monitor and viewing distance. Judge the shapes and the separation between conditions; do not judge the colour or the perceived size.
